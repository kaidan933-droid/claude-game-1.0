    How the car behaves
    • 525 PS flat-six, 7-speed PDK, 0-100 km/h in about 3.4 s, about 290 km/h flat out. DRS opens on its own when you are flat out on a straight.
    • Each tyre has its own load, slip and temperature. Braking or accelerating while cornering eats into lateral grip (friction ellipse); weight shifts under braking, throttle and cornering.
    • Cold tyres grip less; they come up to about 85-95 C after a few corners. Sliding overheats them.
    • Downforce adds grip at speed; crests like Flugplatz and Pflanzgarten unload the car, it can leave the ground. The Karussell is banked.
    • Lifting mid-corner shifts weight forward and the rear-engined car can rotate. Curbs grip a little less, grass and gravel much less.
